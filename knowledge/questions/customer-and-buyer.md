# Questions — customer-and-buyer

Use in customer interviews, case-study mining, sales discovery and win-back conversations. Each question aims at a scene a buyer lived, not an opinion.

- **Q122** How life looked before the problem arrived, what an ordinary day felt like, how the trouble started, and who it touched. — the setup of a case study (Hauge)
- **Q123** What numbers were involved, and what did the company's finances look like before action? — measurable baseline (Hauge)
- **Q124** What single event forced action right then? — the crisis that started the decision (Hauge)
- **Q125** What goal or milestone would prove success, and what earlier solutions failed and why? — the stated finish line and failed attempts (Hauge)
- **Q126** How did you hear about us, what made you skeptical, and what finally made you buy? — the path to decision (Hauge)
- **Q127** How did you feel on day one, what surprised you, what worked best, who supported you, and did results come gradually or suddenly? — the pursuit phase (Hauge)
- **Q128** What was hardest about starting and sticking with it, who discouraged you, did you want to quit, and what inner voice held you back? — conflict and inner fears (Hauge)
- **Q129** What self-talk or support kept you going, and did it echo an old defeat? — inner resilience (Hauge)
- **Q130** When did you know you had succeeded, where were you, who was with you, and what did it feel like? — the climax moment (Hauge)
- **Q131** How do life and relationships compare now to before, and what would you tell others in your position? — aftermath and advice (Hauge)
- **Q132** What circumstances led you to work with us, what was the process like, and how has your life or business changed? — three-question quick screen (Hauge)
- **Q133** What does success look like visually, how will you know the problem is solved, and what result marks the finish line? — clarifying a vague goal (Hauge)
- **Q134** Do your hero's doubts match the four buyer fears: looking foolish, wasting money, proving dreams pointless, or losing identity if successful? — realistic hesitation (Hauge)
- **Q135** What product did you use before ours, and what made you willing to switch? — the switching story (Hall)
- **Q136** Where were you the first time you tried us or first heard of us? — a specific entry scene (Hall)
- **Q137** Have you enjoyed the experience, and how are things now? — current state after use (Hall)
- **Q138** What is your customers' problem, how do they feel, how does it hit their life and business, and what keeps them awake? — the normal before the change (Hall)
- **Q139** How does the product solve the pain, and what does using it feel like? — the explosion in a value story (Hall)
- **Q140** How is life different afterward, which pains vanished, and how do customers feel? — the new normal (Hall)
- **Q141** If you could not mention or show the product, what would you tell customers? — a constraint that forces the story (Hall)
- **Q142** Why do people not choose you, what are the top objections, and which questions do prospects ask most? — the objections to answer in stories (Hall)
- **Q143** Who is your happiest customer, who is your unhappiest, and when did you make one cry or stop one crying? — vivid customer extremes (Hall)
- **Q144** What prompted you to purchase, what problems did you have before, what emotions did the product give you, and what values do you seek in a brand? — survey for emotional material (Mastropierro)
- **Q145** Sum up the experience of using our product. — the customer's own summary line (Mastropierro)
- **Q146** What is the best and worst part of your job? — pain points beneath a price objection (Livesay)
- **Q147** What is the number one thing that would make your boss happy? — career drivers of the buyer (Livesay)
- **Q148** What gives you joy, and are we having fun yet? — rapport and lightness (Livesay)
- **Q149** What do you want to see one month after the project to feel you made a good hire? — success markers (Livesay)
- **Q150** What else do you need to know to feel comfortable choosing me, and if the others agree, can I count on your support? — final concerns and close (Livesay)
- **Q151** What I heard you say is X; is that right? Did my answer fully address it? — confirm listening in Q and A (Livesay)
- **Q152** Does that make sense, and where should we go from here? — close the loop on an objection (Livesay)
- **Q153** It sounds like you are upset because of A, B and C; did I get everything? — surface every grievance of an angry client (Livesay)
- **Q154** Was this a good client, did they pay on time, did we enjoy the work, and was it a big share of revenue? — whether a lost client is worth winning back (Livesay)
- **Q155** What happened next, and what would have happened if this had not worked? — add stakes to a dry case study (Livesay)
- **Q156** Walk me through a recent transaction: how you found the prospect, first contact, resources used, who decided, and how you closed. — bright-spot interview for what works (McKinsey)
- **Q157** Why do customers go out of their way for the brand? — emotional driver such as dignity and respect (Salmon)
- **Q158** What do buyers think of the category leader versus us, what barriers block purchase, what are their top pain points, and whose story is not being told? — insight mining (Quesenberry)
- **Q159** What job does the product do for them? — the customer's underlying purpose (Quesenberry)
- **Q160** What do satisfied and unsatisfied customers feel, and what single human emotion unites them? — a universal truth for the brand (Rodriguez)
- **Q161** What do buyers already know about us and about our solution, and who are they? — funnel discovery (Butler)
- **Q162** For a client story: how was life before, what pushed them to act, how was the experience, and what measurable result followed? — before, trigger, during, after (Butler)
- **Q163** What emotions do customers feel while buying, and what fear do they have about our product? — emotional map and fear (Butler)
- **Q164** What keeps them up at night, and what happens when you ask a plain, naive question directly? — non-obvious barriers such as embarrassment (Kamath)
- **Q165** Do we need this, are you the right person to deliver it, and can we work with you? — the three silent questions of a sales audience (Callahan)
- **Q166** Why did you join this company, when did it go well past its duty to put a problem right, and what do you think is good about what you sell? — sales conviction (Dolan)
- **Q167** How long have you worked here, what did you do before, and what is the weirdest or best thing that happened at work? — drawing stories from service staff (Leitman)
- **Q168** Give an example of when you helped people. — checking whether a candidate answers with a real story or a generality (Maxwell)
- **Q169** How did you feel when you walked in, why, and how did staff interactions change the experience? — a store or service walk-through (Callahan)
