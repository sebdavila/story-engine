# Questions — personal-memory

Use to dig stories out of the teller's own life, including age-banded memoir prompts, photographs, objects and family interviews. Ask one at a time and push for a concrete scene, not a summary.

- **Q001** Which moments could you retell on request, the ones friends ask you to repeat? — your best-known stories (Bowles)
- **Q002** When was the first, last, worst or best time you did something, and how did it differ from every other time? — a moment with built-in contrast (Bowles; Dicks)
- **Q003** When did you break a routine, make a hard choice, or choose to do nothing? — decision points with stakes (Bowles)
- **Q004** When did you change a belief, discover you were wrong, or try to be someone you are not? — an internal turn (Bowles; Lazer)
- **Q005** If your life were a film, which scene is burned into your memory, and what did you see, hear, smell, touch, say and think while inside it? — a sensory anchor scene (Bowles)
- **Q006** Finish the line: I used to ___, but now I ___. — a before-and-after arc in one sentence (Bowles; James)
- **Q007** Finish: I used to believe ___, but then I learned ___. — a changed belief with its trigger (James)
- **Q008** Finish: I used to think... — a belief that later cracked (Apps)
- **Q009** Which object matters to you, how did you get it, and what would losing it mean? — meaning carried by an object (Bowles)
- **Q010** Who is in your web of people, places and activities, and which link hides a story? — story seeds across a life (Bowles)
- **Q011** Which tale does everyone tell about you, what risk do you regret not taking, and which single day would you live again? — reflective, late-life material (Bowles)
- **Q012** What single moment made today different from every earlier day, if you had five minutes onstage about it? — a daily habit that supplies material (Dicks)
- **Q013** Combine Kiss, Car, Pet, Trouble, Injury, Gift or Travel with First, Last, Best, Worst; which entries repeat, which are mere anecdotes, which are full stories? — a fast bank of candidates and a sort between anecdote and story (Dicks)
- **Q014** Does the story hold a five-second moment when something changed for good or your heart moved? — the core of a storyworthy tale (Dicks)
- **Q015** Remove the exotic location or dramatic premise: is anything worth telling left? — whether the story rests on the backdrop or on the person (Dicks)
- **Q016** What do people ask you about most, and what is the messy truth behind it? — the story people already want (Leitman)
- **Q017** Complete: I am ___; I was ___ (childhood fears, odd short-term jobs); ___ is the story of my life. — identity material (Leitman)
- **Q018** What ten quirks do you have, and what incident explains each? — character traits tied to events (Leitman)
- **Q019** Complete: I have been haunted by ___; everywhere I go I ___; I secretly love ___; I secretly hate ___. — honest material between safe and too much (Leitman)
- **Q020** What white lies have you told about your skills or past, and what happened when they were tested? — comic exposure and humility (Leitman)
- **Q021** In your most-told social story, what universal feeling or misconception makes it land with strangers? — the theme already working (Leitman)
- **Q022** What claim do you make about love, dating, travel or money, and which single event proves it? — thesis plus proof (Leitman)
- **Q023** Which ten things, people or places make you react physically, for better or worse? — emotional hot spots (Leitman)
- **Q024** What do you get overly excited about, pretend to care about, pretend not to care about, or fear people will find out? — hidden cares and pretenses (Leitman)
- **Q025** What makes you cry or furious, when did you snap, and what past reaction can you not believe now? — heightened emotion scenes (Leitman)
- **Q026** What small moment or setback turned out to start something much bigger? — a small trigger with large consequences (Leitman)
- **Q027** What day did you expect to be wonderful and it turned out terrible, or the reverse; when were you wrong about a person? — reversal of expectation (Leitman)
- **Q028** Where have you returned, what keeps happening to you, which people do you keep attracting, which family role do you repeat, when did you become your parent? — recurring patterns (Leitman)
- **Q029** Have you had an overblown reaction, a heated fight over something trivial, or gone to absurd lengths to prove a point? — the benign, funny story (Leitman)
- **Q030** Where was your self-esteem then, what were you wearing, what music played, what car, who were your idols, what did you eat? — period and sensory specifics (Leitman)
- **Q031** Complete: I'm the type who will ___; I never ___; but I always ___. Which five words describe you, and which five things do you want? — self-definition as character (Leitman)
- **Q032** When did you win something unexpected, or lose and gain something anyway; what is your biggest strength and flaw? — underdog and flaw material (Leitman)
- **Q033** What did you do before this role, and what made you choose it? — origin and motive (Leitman; Livesay)
- **Q034** Tell about a time you did something you never thought you would, tried to be someone you are not, made a tough call, changed a key relationship, or had a secret exposed. — story-slam prompts (Lazer)
- **Q035** Which memory grabs your attention, what was your role, what crisis did you face, what tilted it, what did you learn, how do you live differently, and what must you cut? — shaping a memory into a story (James)
- **Q036** Complete: I wish I could warn my younger self that ___; the biggest lesson from my father was ___; I am secretly ___. — memory starters (James)
- **Q037** Can you summarize the arc as: I used to ___ until ___; since then I have ___? — arc in three beats (James)
- **Q038** When did you feel like an imposter, or live through a move, firing, wedding or other first? — recall triggers (James)
- **Q039** What shaped you more than anything else, and what repeated frustrations or elations led to the work you do? — patterns that forged a life direction (Simmons)
- **Q040** Which good or bad results of past efforts changed how you work and relate to people? — consequences that shaped habits (Simmons)
- **Q041** What was your biggest mistake, painful crisis or career turning point, and what would you do differently? — lessons with stakes (Simmons)
- **Q042** When did you last cry, dance for joy, or want to crawl under the table? — vulnerability moments (Simmons)
- **Q043** Why is your favorite book or film your favorite, and how would you retell it from your view? — borrowed meaning you can own (Simmons)
- **Q044** What is the first image that comes to mind from that period, which single sense dominates it, and what ordinary moment now glows? — a gleaming detail (Buster)
- **Q045** Who was a force of nature in your life, who were you before them, what fear did you show through inaction, and what everyday act proved you changed? — a personal antagonist and transformation (Buster)
- **Q046** What do you love, when did it start, which moment hooked you, how did life change, and how would you explain it to a novice? — a passion story (Buster)
- **Q047** What is the bare factual spine of the event and the hidden emotional truth under it? — fact versus feeling (Buster)
- **Q048** Think of a challenge you overcame: what process, advice, mentor or idea got you through? — resilience story (Karia)
- **Q049** What do you already love, and which skills do you have rather than lack? — strengths when stuck for a topic (Miller)
- **Q050** Which memory does this random word bring up, and how does another person recall the same event? — shared past, two views (Miller)
- **Q051** What is your greatest struggle, what makes you guilty, and how did life turn out differently than expected? — emotional openers (Lazer)
- **Q052** What did you want to be as a child, what lesson came from a parent, friend or dog, and what tiny decision reshaped your life? — warm, lightly funny entries (Biesenbach; Lazer)
- **Q053** Which stories moved you, read or seen, and what does that say about you? — values shown through taste (Forman)
- **Q054** Which mentor, family member, teacher or boss shaped who you are, and which challenge forced choices and insight? — signature-story seeds (Forman)
- **Q055** What do you want to be famous for, and what flag are you planting? — identity claim to prove with a story (Maestas)
- **Q056** Which three things annoy you most right now, and which one holds a comic premise? — humor seeds from irritation (Cody)
- **Q057** What is your most embarrassing story? — a humbling, relatable anecdote (Cody; Hall)
- **Q058** What did you love or hate that you could exaggerate into a worst-case opening? — comic scene seed (Haven)
- **Q059** Who holds your hand in a mental picture, what is their expression, and what happened? — guided recall (Haven)
- **Q060** Which three things go wrong for a given character, place and activity, and who suffers most? — trouble inventory for fiction (Haven)
- **Q061** What story do you want to tell, which problems should it solve, and what interests you enough to cost you sleep? — a personal theme (Terenzzo)
- **Q062** Describe your school's floor plan: which teacher or story is most vivid? — childhood setting memory (Apps)
- **Q063** What was your greatest childhood fear, favorite toy or book, and happiest or saddest holiday? — early-years material (Apps)
- **Q064** Which unwritten family rules existed and when did you break one? — teen conflict with authority (Apps)
- **Q065** Describe your first paid job, driving test, first love, heroes and best friends. — teen-years anchors (Apps)
- **Q066** How did you meet your partner, and what were your first home and first year together like? — young-adult and partnership scenes (Apps)
- **Q067** What incident will your partner never let you forget, and did family disapprove? — partnership comedy and tension (Apps)
- **Q068** Which achievement are you proudest of, and which toughest problem can you write as a story? — early-career stories (Apps)
- **Q069** What happened in layoffs, job shifts or family crises? — career upheaval (Apps)
- **Q070** How did relationships with parents and siblings change, and how did it feel when children left home? — middle-years change (Apps)
- **Q071** What were your feelings at retirement, which health challenges did you face, and which places would you revisit or avoid? — later-years reflection (Apps)
- **Q072** Which sacred place, life-changing person or watershed milestone belongs in the book? — special anchors (Apps)
- **Q073** Which five annoyances could become a story, and how would someone else describe you? — free-writing starters (Apps)
- **Q074** What would you write for five minutes about the color green, fresh bread, thunder, loneliness or silence? — sensory free-writing (Apps)
- **Q075** Replace Do you remember...? with Tell me what I was like. Then ask: Can you remember any more? What is an example? Is there a story about that? — interview probes that open answers (Apps)
- **Q076** From a childhood age, stepping back in one- or two-year jumps, what is the first concrete scene, what triggered it, who was there, what shifted, and why did it stick? — thinking backward to find scenes (Maguire)
- **Q077** Pick one emotion word from a list of sixty: what is the first scene it recalls? — emotion-led recall (Maguire)
- **Q078** Who was your childhood hero, which traits did you copy, what tested that loyalty, and where does the hero live in you now? — a formative influence (Maguire)
- **Q079** At each five-year milestone, what outside pressures, inner desires and missing information shaped the choice, who helped or blocked, and what if you had taken the other road? — a crossroads decision (Maguire)
- **Q080** In a photograph, who is present, what do faces and postures show, how far apart do they stand, who took it, what came before and after, and what sound or smell surrounded it? — interrogating a picture for story (Maguire)
- **Q081** What do you know about family origins and migrations, chores and rituals, mischief, courtship, proudest turning points, and odd relatives or heirlooms? — family interview clusters (Maguire)
- **Q082** For each scar, illness or physical habit, when did it appear, which event caused it, and how did it change how you move through the world? — the body as map of stories (Maguire)
- **Q083** What did the crisis change, who acted, what sensory detail stood out, what conflict grew, and what small turning point or act of endurance followed? — building a crisis story (Maguire)
- **Q084** What do listeners already carry into the room, why does this story belong to them now, and are you offering connection or venting? — fit of a personal story to its audience (Maguire)
- **Q085** Are there living descendants nearby whom this story could embarrass? — family ethics check (Apps)
- **Q086** Whose eyes see this scene, and could someone else tell it differently? — perspective check for family accounts (Apps; Miller)
- **Q087** What is this story about in one or two sentences, and are the scenes in the right order? — revision of a memoir draft (Apps)
- **Q088** What is the central point of this scene? — scene-level purpose test (Apps)
- **Q089** How would you describe yourself through someone else's eyes, and which three people changed your life? — outside views of self (Apps)
- **Q090** What do you love about your own work, what does your child think you do, and what frustrates and delights you? — everyday-life interviews (Biesenbach)
- **Q091** What was your worst job, and what did it teach you? — a humble source of anecdotes (Apps)
