# Questions — founder-and-origin

Use for founder stories, pitches, investor and recruiting narratives, and any story about why a person or company exists.

- **Q092** Which role do I play in this story, where did the dream begin, why am I taking the risk, and what vision do I want to pass on? — three origin anchors for a founder (Terenzzo)
- **Q093** When in your childhood or early life did you first love this craft? — the root of the calling (Hall)
- **Q094** When did you realize there has to be a better way, what were you feeling, who was there, and how did it unfold? — the founding insight moment (Hall)
- **Q095** When was everything going wrong in the early days, and what struggle led to eventual victory? — the dark stretch before the win (Hall)
- **Q096** Can this founder overcome adversity, are they committed, and are they emotionally invested? — the questions investors silently ask (Hall)
- **Q097** Tell me about a time you knew you had really nailed something. — a moment of certainty (Livesay)
- **Q098** What lesson did you learn in that experience? — the business takeaway hidden in a personal story (Livesay)
- **Q099** What world are you creating, and do I want to be part of it? — the two filters of an investor (Livesay)
- **Q100** Why you, and why now? — proof of unique fit and market timing (Livesay)
- **Q101** What inspired you to enter this profession, or what did you do before this firm? — a memorable one-line introduction (Livesay)
- **Q102** What are you willing to do that your competition is not? — a preparation or service advantage (Livesay)
- **Q103** What is the true story of the company's founding: what customer need, who noticed it, how was it different, what early challenge was beaten, and which early truths still guide it? — an origin story (Edwards)
- **Q104** How did you start your company, what obstacles did you meet, and what inspiring thing can you share? — a personal brand tale (Mastropierro)
- **Q105** How did you get started, what was Plan A, what obstacles hit, and which client success drives you? — business origin (Leitman)
- **Q106** What is the founder's story or struggle, and why does the company exist beyond money? — founder and purpose (Quesenberry)
- **Q107** Where did you begin humbly, what was your call-to-adventure moment, who pulled you out of the hole, which trials proved grit, and how did you change? — a sales-style hero's journey (Lazer)
- **Q108** What is your North Star, and why are you pursuing this venture? — purpose tied to lived experience (Kamath)
- **Q109** What are the top reasons not to fund or buy from us, and what were your worst mistakes? — surfacing weaknesses before an investor does (Kamath)
- **Q110** Why am I pitching, and why go this far? — replaces what the investor wants to see with real motive (Kamath)
- **Q111** Is this a great measure of who I am, what makes me uniquely qualified, and why do I want to tell it? — fit and motive check before telling (Maestas)
- **Q112** Is your goal to look like the hero, or to build something lasting by helping others win? — ego versus service (Maestas)
- **Q113** Why did you get involved in this work, why this organization, and what was your proudest moment as a member? — shared purpose for divided teams (Guptara)
- **Q114** What is unusual about you, what experiences made you you, and what is important to you? — the WHO and WHY of a personal pitch (Guptara)
- **Q115** Why are you trustworthy, what is your superpower, and when did you show honesty or perseverance in one incident? — trust evidence (Guptara)
- **Q116** Why do you want to be successful, and why at this? — motive under a goal (Guptara)
- **Q117** What is your brand's superpower and mission? — a brand's heroic core (Butler)
- **Q118** What do you want to be famous for, who primarily serves as your bullseye audience, and what action must the story trigger? — focus before a founder narrative (Maestas)
- **Q119** What would you do with five million dollars and one year, and what is your greatest struggle? — motive interview for a founder (Lazer)
- **Q120** What does each team member's career change tell about why they joined? — a team page with real stories (Livesay)
- **Q121** What do you want people to notice you noticing? — a personal observation habit that makes you vivid (Guptara)
