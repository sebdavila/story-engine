# Questions — delivery-and-performance

Use to prepare a live telling, a talk or a meeting, and to check the teller's readiness, humor and mindset.

- **Q611** What are my intentions, can I let them go and put the audience's needs first, and how does that feel? — emotional briefing before a meeting (Edwards)
- **Q612** When are you most yourself, and when in the past week did you feel authentic? — knowing yourself as a teller (Edwards)
- **Q613** What are you good at, what challenges have you overcome with whose help, and what motivates you? — strengths, hurdles, motives (Edwards)
- **Q614** Where are you going in five and ten years, and who benefits from what you do? — direction and difference made (Edwards)
- **Q615** If stuck, which people, places and turning-point moments mattered most? — fallback mining (Edwards)
- **Q616** What do you want people to notice you noticing, and where can you speak more plainly instead of hiding behind professionalism? — plain speech (Guptara)
- **Q617** Where could a recent report, talk or meeting have done better with a story? — spotting story opportunities (Guptara)
- **Q618** What recently happened to you, what were you struggling with, who helped, and what are you grateful for? — conversational material (Guptara)
- **Q619** Who am I, what do I care about, is my internal narrative holding me back, and do the stories I tell myself match the ones I tell others? — self-narrative alignment (Guptara)
- **Q620** Do I blame myself, others or circumstances for failure, and is my definition of success my own? — mindset check (Guptara)
- **Q621** How do I begin to believe I can make a difference? — hope self-check (Simmons)
- **Q622** What identity do you want, which topics excite you, which mediums fit, how often will you tell, and what are the cue, craving, response and reward? — a storytelling habit (Lazer)
- **Q623** What does this story say about me, and what do you infer about me? — story buddy feedback (Callahan)
- **Q624** What do you think is the point of the story, and what does it mean for you? — meaning check by listener (Callahan)
- **Q625** Is what you say compatible with what I know, is it coherent and plausible, is the source credible, and do others believe it? — how a skeptical listener judges (Callahan)
- **Q626** How is this like what happens at work? — analogy mining (Callahan)
- **Q627** What story best explains the decision or change we are making? — a clarity story (Callahan)
- **Q628** What does that story mean to you? — encoding a story in memory (Callahan)
- **Q629** How authentic are you willing to be, warts and all? — sanitized facade audit (Cody)
- **Q630** How is the crowd, and how much would be too much for this group? — read the room and calibrate vulnerability (Cody)
- **Q631** What will I say and how will I say it if called to the stage? — a stand-up routine draft (Cody)
- **Q632** What is wrong with this picture, how does it make me feel, and what do I wish I could change? — original comic thesis (Cody)
- **Q633** Does she know she is going to be roasted? — consent before put-down humor (Cody)
- **Q634** What is your body communicating, and how loudly must you project to reach everyone? — delivery as message (Parikh)
- **Q635** Are you dressed in line with your role and the audience's expectations, and how loudly must you project? — presence (Parikh)
