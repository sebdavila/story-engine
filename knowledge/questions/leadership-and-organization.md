# Questions — leadership-and-organization

Use to find leader, team and company stories (values, change, purpose, culture) and to plan story use in an initiative.

- **Q170** What is one of the hardest choices you have made, and what three events shaped who you are? — character and connection stories (Callahan)
- **Q171** When did you put people before results, and when were you surprised by what you care about? — a caring story (Callahan)
- **Q172** When has your purpose been clear and strong, and when did you walk away because your values clashed with a group's? — purpose stories (Callahan)
- **Q173** When did you say I will never do that again, and what is the most important lesson you learned? — lesson stories (Callahan)
- **Q174** Have you seen people come from behind to win or turn a bad situation around? — inspiration stories (Callahan)
- **Q175** When did you put your reputation on the line for a greater cause? — courage story (Callahan)
- **Q176** When did a small innovation make a big difference, and when was your team exceptional against the odds? — innovation and collaboration (Callahan)
- **Q177** What stood out today, did I hear or read a good story, and when might I tell it? — a daily story-spotting journal (Callahan)
- **Q178** How did you get your funding; what happened there? — a project retrospective that elicits moments (Callahan)
- **Q179** When did a small thing make a difference, where were you, what was happening around you, and what was said? — picture-forming coaching prompts (Callahan)
- **Q180** Does anyone have a story from the last few days about something special for a guest? — a daily pre-shift success story (Callahan)
- **Q181** What was significant for you in this story, does it remind you of anything, could it happen here, and what would you need to start? — a story discussion call (Callahan)
- **Q182** When did the organization overcome a daunting obstacle, what did people feel, which episodes mattered, and what lesson changed it? — overcoming-challenges story (Edwards)
- **Q183** When did the organization need to change, which beliefs and processes shifted, what early attempts failed, what tipped it, and who championed it? — navigating-change story (Edwards)
- **Q184** In a few sentences, what does each value mean, who embodies it, how does the company react when it is not met, and when did it take a short-term loss to protect it? — values story (Edwards)
- **Q185** What is the final aim of your work, how does it alter customers' lives, which projects embody it, and what quest is still ahead? — mission story (Edwards)
- **Q186** When is your team at its best, what drives it, and how does it make a difference? — team story-mining (Edwards)
- **Q187** What have you been foisting on people as a foregone conclusion, and how could they write their own lottery ticket? — buying-in through co-creation (Guptara)
- **Q188** When did you realize the old strategy would no longer work, why did you have it, and what forced the change? — a strategy pivot story (Guptara)
- **Q189** What unspoken objections do people have about this decision, and how could a story disarm them? — a pre-empt story (Guptara)
- **Q190** Which departments could use a story bank, who maintains it, can everyone add, do stories need approval, and are some sections eyes-only? — running an organizational story bank (Guptara)
- **Q191** Does the company know how it began, its past setbacks, its biggest accounts and failures, and its comebacks? — a culture audit (Hall)
- **Q192** When have I seen our true purpose in action, and when did I first learn this lesson? — purpose-story mining (Hall)
- **Q193** What does this change mean for me, how will it affect me, why should I care about yet another change, and why change because you say so? — the employee's view of change (Dolan)
- **Q194** In each role, what successes or awards are you proud of, and what struggle did you work through? — triumph mining (Dolan)
- **Q195** Which decision turned out wrong, what did you fail to do for lack of courage, which relationship broke, and what mistake taught you? — tragedy mining (Dolan)
- **Q196** How did you come to each job and why leave, when did you choose between two values or loyalties, and which transitions were forced, chosen or started by you? — tension and transition mining (Dolan)
- **Q197** Across your story list, do you have too many of one type, such as triumph, and too few of another? — inventory balance (Dolan)
- **Q198** Does the listener feel compelled to buy, back the change, accept the role, believe you, follow you or respect you? — the audience decision you want (Dolan)
- **Q199** What story or stories will move the initiative forward, and who tells it best in the organization? — start of a strategic initiative (Forman)
- **Q200** Whose support is essential, whose can you afford to lose, and what will push people to resist or support the story of the future? — mapping stakeholders (Forman)
- **Q201** How will length, point of view, detail and language change for each stakeholder group? — tailoring versions (Forman)
- **Q202** What are the pulse points of the story: where to begin, which middle moments to stress, and the end point? — shaping the arc of an initiative story (Forman)
- **Q203** Do culture and reporting lines help or hinder influence? Which parts of the initiative are negotiable? — fit to the organization (Forman)
- **Q204** Can the story be cut into sequential chapters, what data goes with it, who has credibility to champion it, and in what order do you seek support? — rollout plan (Forman)
- **Q205** What purpose do you want in story form, with whom, and why now? — the right time and aim (Forman)
- **Q206** Who should tell the story, and who should appear in it? — teller and cast (Forman)
- **Q207** What are you most passionate about, which defining moment involved it, and how does it tie back to work? — the Me part of an employee story (Forman)
- **Q208** What part do you play in giving customers an outstanding experience, and what do the company's service values mean for you? — My Work (Forman)
- **Q209** What makes you proud of the company, and what is your favorite story of it helping a person or community? — My Company (Forman)
- **Q210** How do you or your team give back, and why does it matter to you? — My Commitment (Forman)
- **Q211** Who do we become if we get this right, who are our customers and what do they gain, what are the risks of acting or holding back, and how long will it take? — strategic North Star questions (Forman)
- **Q212** Is the message understood, which parts move people, does it fit the brand promise, do stakeholders believe we can deliver, and does it differentiate us? — external validation of a brand story (Forman)
- **Q213** What is the hot sauce, the sensory detail that lets listeners see and feel the scene? — vivid detail (Forman)
- **Q214** Where are the novel aha moments, and have you removed jargon and clichés? — freshness (Forman)
- **Q215** Which photo triggers a patient or customer experience you can tell? — image as story trigger (Forman)
- **Q216** When rehearsing with colleagues who play the audience, when are they interested, bored, confused or resistant? — rehearsal feedback (Forman)
- **Q217** Is silence killing your company; which conflicts and employee stories go unspoken? — suppressed stories (Salmon)
- **Q218** Who produces these stories, through which channels do they spread, and how do they claim legitimacy? — auditing an institutional narrative (Salmon)
- **Q219** Does your company make decisions only from the top down, and do employees feel free to speak or just avoid being fired? — culture check (Cody)
- **Q220** Do colleagues give me oxygen or take it away? — energy of teammates (Cody)
- **Q221** Who would you want to quarantine with, and what was your craziest video-call moment? — remote team bonding (Cody)
- **Q222** Who do I work with who communicates well, who leaves people feeling a meeting went well, who listens well, and with whom do I often misunderstand? — mindset reflection on colleagues (Edwards)
- **Q223** What is my own part in those misunderstandings, and how could I communicate better with them? — self-reflection on conflict (Edwards)
- **Q224** Do your stories demonstrate the values you profess, and how many can you find? — whether values are lived (Simmons)
