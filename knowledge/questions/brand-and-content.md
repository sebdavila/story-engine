# Questions — brand-and-content

Use for brand narrative, positioning, social and video content, and content strategy. Ask them of a brief, a campaign idea or a published piece.

- **Q272** Why does the brand exist beyond profit, and does the customer appear as the hero? — mission and hero audit (Rodriguez)
- **Q273** Without the logo, would audiences still recognize this storyline as ours and link it to our universal truth? — no-logo test (Rodriguez)
- **Q274** What is the central theme, which stakeholder needs and benefits does it serve, how will it run, and why does the business tell it? — napkin pitch (Rodriguez)
- **Q275** How does the story look from each department, is it simple to use daily, and does it evoke the intended emotion? — internal assimilation (Rodriguez)
- **Q276** Does the story affirm core values, build trust with all stakeholders, show inclusive behavior and honor commitments to customers? — ethical checkpoints (Rodriguez)
- **Q277** Does this employee or influencer look like a natural character; stripped of the brand, would they still draw this audience? — champion fit (Rodriguez)
- **Q278** Why do you feel that way about the story? Ask it seven times in a row after prototype feedback. — root emotional drivers (Rodriguez)
- **Q279** For mission, audience, character, plot, conclusion and structure: who, what, where, how, and why asked twice? — definition matrix (Rodriguez)
- **Q280** Where, when, who, what starts the conflict, how does the character struggle, what cues set the mood, what values remain? — story map (Quesenberry)
- **Q281** What does the company stand for, what bigger problem does it solve, what cause or community does it serve, and what is it dedicated to being best at? — Why discovery (Quesenberry)
- **Q282** For which segment, for what use, and better than which competitor because of what evidence? — positioning sentence (Quesenberry)
- **Q283** Whose eyes are you looking through when you view the world? — point-of-view insight (Quesenberry)
- **Q284** Why communicate, to whom, what do they think, feel and do now, what do we want them to, what is the single most persuasive idea, why believe it, what tone, what deliverables? — creative brief (Quesenberry)
- **Q285** What vital management questions must each objective answer before you choose a dashboard metric? — key performance questions (Quesenberry)
- **Q286** What is the essence, hero, audience, conflict, setting, stakes and tone, in 25 words or fewer? — core concept (Paulsen)
- **Q287** Why do this, what real benefit, who exactly, what theme, what genre rules, who guides, how do we connect, what lasting change? — core creative strategy questions (Paulsen)
- **Q288** Does this matter to this audience now, can they see themselves in it, and does every rule obey the world's logic? — relevance, relatability, believability (Paulsen)
- **Q289** What can the audience do (watch, explore, interact, influence, co-create) with which part of the world? — audience role (Paulsen)
- **Q290** For every guest-facing touchpoint, who staffs, maintains, triggers and pays for it backstage? — pairing front and back stage (Paulsen)
- **Q291** Instead of did you like it, if a friend asked what happened, how would you describe it? — neutral test questions (Paulsen)
- **Q292** Does the story invent a victim, cast an outside group as enemy, alter history, overload conspiracies, oversimplify to absolutes, invoke a grand cause, weaponize nostalgia, or excuse any means? — hidden agenda audit (Paulsen)
- **Q293** Who are we, and does every post confirm it? — brand consistency (Vaynerchuk)
- **Q294** If I saw this picture, would I share it? — shareability gate (Vaynerchuk)
- **Q295** At what times do we get the most response, what happens with slang, a different tagline on the same image, a hashtag, or an animated GIF? — platform testing variables (Vaynerchuk)
- **Q296** Is the post text too long, provocative or surprising, is the photo striking, is the logo visible, is the call to action well placed, is it interesting to anyone, and are we asking too much? — Facebook check (Vaynerchuk)
- **Q297** Is the tweet to the point, the hashtag unique, the image sharp, and the voice authentic? — Twitter check (Vaynerchuk)
- **Q298** Does the picture feed the consumer's dream, do boards have clever titles, is the price shown, does each photo link out, and could the pin double as a magazine photo? — Pinterest check (Vaynerchuk)
- **Q299** Is the image artsy enough, are hashtags descriptive, and do stories appeal to the young? — Instagram check (Vaynerchuk)
- **Q300** Does the theme reflect the brand, and is there a fun animated GIF? — Tumblr check (Vaynerchuk)
- **Q301** Is there enough brain stimulation here, are you making people care? — care check (Creek)
- **Q302** Who are you in 25 words, who are the curious viewers and what stops them, and who are three types your videos are not for? — brand clarity (Creek)
- **Q303** Is the video based on who your audience really is rather than their online fantasy, and could it prompt we were just talking about this? — reality check (Creek)
- **Q304** Does your 25-word statement say what and why, inspire action, let others explain you, use your words and name who you talk to? — five-point clarity test (Creek)
- **Q305** Is this great creative idea also great for the business? — lane alignment (Creek)
- **Q306** Picture two real people mentioning your brand: who, how do they know each other, what do they discuss, where, and what blocks them? — customer conversation mining (Creek)
- **Q307** What do you do that others do not, where are you that others are not, why did followers pick you, and what are your ambition, risk, plan, difference and stand? — rules of engagement discovery (Creek)
- **Q308** Name the hero and the villain, the adversity faced, whether it finished in a win or a loss, the takeaway, and the link between the struggle and what the viewer lives through. — story-element check (Creek)
- **Q309** If a video flopped, were viewers leaving in seconds or forgetting the point, and was the audience wrong, the lane wrong, or the stand too weak? — underperformance diagnosis (Creek)
- **Q310** Do consumers see a coherent brand narrative, and what if we assume they do not until shown otherwise? — presence check (Vincent)
- **Q311** Describe a high point from this area of life: the people present, the sensory details that linger, and the part the brand played. — peeling the onion in research (Vincent)
- **Q312** Which heroes and villains populate our world, where and when is it set, what one dramatic question drives the plot, and why does our worldview matter? — brand story bible (Vincent)
- **Q313** What value changes by the end, and what concrete cause produces the change? — controlling idea (Vincent)
- **Q314** Which ritual artifacts are essential or interchangeable, what script do consumers follow, what roles do they play, and who validates? — ritual diagnostic (Vincent)
- **Q315** What benefits, value exchanges, obligations and boundaries define each partner, and does the consumer sit above both? — partnership alignment (Vincent)
- **Q316** In three years, in future tense, what dramatic polarity separates hope from failure? — turnaround story (Vincent)
- **Q317** What does the brand's story say about its place in the market, and what story should the whole audience know? — market positioning (Butler)
- **Q318** What stories do we already tell well, which of four brand story types comes first, and what primal urges and emotions can improve them? — campaign strategy (Butler)
- **Q319** What commonalities hold our micro-tribe, which language and rituals mark it, and how can we increase members' socializing? — tribal cohesion (Butler)
- **Q320** What is the tribe's primary urge and how does it affect buying? — urge mining (Butler)
- **Q321** Which brand character and two archetypes fit our value stories, and which plot structures can we test in the next 90 days? — voice and plot (Butler)
- **Q322** What must happen just before the target outcome? — backward planning (Butler)
- **Q323** What are our personas interested in beyond the product, what do they share culturally, and which FAQs do they face? — non-promotional content mining (Butler)
- **Q324** Am I holding back my best work for fear of judgment, does the plan cover 90 days, and is this short because the message needs it or because short is assumed to win? — self-check (Butler)
- **Q325** Does your company have an original story honest, captivating and unique enough that people would pay to join it? — differentiation amid clutter (Salmon)
- **Q326** What is in a name: what quality defines the company beyond its logo? — brand essence (Salmon)
- **Q327** Are you addressing consumers or participants? — passive versus narrative co-participation (Salmon)
- **Q328** What is the name of your thing, is it used the same everywhere, and do you introduce it early and confidently? — Hero check in a speed story (Cowie)
- **Q329** What list of things you do shares one overarching arc, can anyone grasp it without jargon, and does it match what you want to be known for? — What (Cowie)
- **Q330** Does the setting establish your space quickly, use familiar markets or problems, and stay specific but broad enough? — Where (Cowie)
- **Q331** Which past experience, collaboration or timeframe of research shows credibility, and what familiar success can you connect to? — When (Cowie)
- **Q332** What is the headline how in one sentence, what are the main steps, and what is open to feedback? — How (Cowie)
- **Q333** Why is your hero acting, what stakeholder sub-whys apply, and does it sound sincere read aloud to a skeptic? — Why (Cowie)
- **Q334** Can you merge the six answers into one flowing paragraph of about 50 words that works as an email opener? — assembly (Cowie)
- **Q335** What action should the audience take, is it clear, and how can it feel low-risk? — engagement (Cowie)
- **Q336** Who can refine the story, where are the gaps, and in what order will you involve others? — collaboration (Cowie)
- **Q337** Are the six blocks specific yet flexible, how will you revisit them, and what is your first bold act today? — momentum (Cowie)
- **Q338** How do we stay fresh and meaningful to new audiences? — brand north star (Snow)
- **Q339** If someone searches for an answer, would they rather reach your piece than anything else online? — publication gate (Snow)
- **Q340** Where does the audience spend time, why are we creating content, and what does success look like for the business? — bull's-eye channels and KPIs (Snow)
- **Q341** How far do people read, do they read more than one, and what do winning stories share? — optimization (Snow)
- **Q342** Which phrases do people search on this topic and which formats win on social? — data-driven discovery (Snow)
- **Q343** How would a favorite writer write this paragraph, and how could I make it half as long? — style and cutting (Snow)
- **Q344** Do you currently sell on production cost or perceived value, on features or benefits, and do you cast yourself as hero or as the guide? — positioning review (Guptara)
- **Q345** What villain can you and your readers fight together? How does the story align with the goal of the copy? — copy shaping (Mastropierro)
- **Q346** What do we want to be famous for, and how do the people we serve describe our work in their own words? — authentic language (Maestas)
- **Q347** Does the content convey the spirit and values of the brand, look to the future, create a bond, and avoid cold technical phrasing? — Web voice (Forman)
- **Q348** Does a credible tagline frame diverse stories, and do they show the company delivering on its promise with accurate data and emotion? — brand promise proof (Forman)
- **Q349** What do you want to be known for, and does the story translate our differentiator and brand value? — strategic alignment (Terenzzo)
- **Q350** Suppose you had paid for access to this story: would you pass it on to a friend or demand a refund? — entertainment test (Terenzzo)
- **Q351** Can this 30-second ad grow into a film or a series? — depth of an ad concept (Terenzzo)
- **Q352** Who is the villain, and does it have agency rather than being dirt or a rival brand? — antagonist for an ad (Terenzzo)
- **Q353** What is the protagonist's surname, which weekday does the journey start, and what are the parents' full names? — checking a writer built a real world (Terenzzo)
- **Q354** Whose story is this, and who has something to learn? — the human protagonist behind a product story (Terenzzo)
- **Q355** What can this product or service do for people, what can people do with it, and does the project show change and give a clear call to action? — action audit (Lupton)
- **Q356** In what way do users participate, are they free to roam or guided along a route, how does the story open and close, and where does it peak? — user journey (Lupton)
- **Q357** Does the project carry one dominant mood or one that shifts; where are the highs, lows, pain points and rewards? — emotional curve of an experience (Lupton)
- **Q358** Picture the product as a person: what would it say, how would it move, and who do we picture owning it? — product persona (Lupton)
- **Q359** Does giving a product a gender win a particular audience, or does it risk pushing others away? — persona risk (Lupton)
- **Q360** Do Gestalt groupings make the visual journey clear, can a user with a task find what they need, and do color, texture and form carry non-visual senses? — sensation audit (Lupton)
- **Q361** Who are you helping, what are their aspirations, what blocks them, and what analogy expresses it? — design audience (Sandler)
- **Q362** What sign vehicles, such as settings, appearance and manners, shape the design story? — signs (Sandler)
- **Q363** Does the work meet standards for usability, efficiency, safety, accessibility, transparency, restrained persuasion, defense against bad actors, life impact, ecological impact and delight? — ten ethics checks (Sandler)
- **Q364** What happens after happily ever after: when does relevance peak, and what new needs emerge? — life after delivery (Sandler)
- **Q365** What barely perceptible moment defines the design's emotional aboutness against look-alikes? — the infrathin moment (Sandler)
- **Q366** Are the channels of your storytelling network consistent, or do they show a Jekyll and Hyde? — channel consistency (Sandler)
- **Q367** Is this a basic story, a performance story or a WOW story, or a progression through all three? — level of the design story (Sandler)
- **Q368** What is the purpose of the design, how will it break new ground, what distinct value does it promise, and why are you the right builder? — design pitch (Sandler)
- **Q369** Which resources are valuable, rare, hard to imitate and non-substitutable? — durable advantages (Sandler)
- **Q370** What is the design's tone, the emotional attitude, versus its tenor, the constant subject? — tone versus tenor (Sandler)
