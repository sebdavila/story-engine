# Questions — plot-and-scene

Use to plan or test plot structure, scenes, dialogue, setting and sensory detail.

- **Q572** Which facts are needed to grasp this event, and what can I reveal beforehand to magnify its effect on this person now? — setup for a hammer moment (Rubin)
- **Q573** Do my central question, medium and main idea align? — alignment (Rubin)
- **Q574** After the question is answered, what lingering questions remain, and which single closing image captures the right final emotion? — wrap-up (Rubin)
- **Q575** What happened right before the scene, what does each speaker need, and what reaction do they expect? — dialogue context (Rubin)
- **Q576** What five to ten hidden meanings could Let's get a cup of coffee carry? — subtext exercise (Rubin)
- **Q577** Which five stories do I love most, what genre is my own life, which medium suits my main conflict layer, and which conventions must I honor or twist? — genre and medium fit (Rubin)
- **Q578** What topic at a dull party makes my blood run hot, and why would someone with my biography write this story? — big-game mining (Rubin)
- **Q579** What does the reader need to know right now to understand this scene? — information control (Barker)
- **Q580** Is this the right direction for the story next, and how does this scene move plot or character forward? — scene necessity (Barker)
- **Q581** Can I move this scene elsewhere without collapsing a wing of the story? — dependency test (Barker)
- **Q582** Did the protagonist end the scene with a new emotion, knowledge or a worse predicament? — change per scene (Barker)
- **Q583** At every major plot point, did the result better the protagonist's position or heighten tension and stakes? — escalation (Barker)
- **Q584** What is the main trajectory, and what complicating secondary trajectory? — plot lines (Barker)
- **Q585** Why today; why does the story begin at this moment rather than any other? — the starting moment (Barker)
- **Q586** What does Act One prime the audience to expect, and do the actions of Act 2A support that goal? — promise and delivery (D'Costa)
- **Q587** Who am I, where am I, when is it, and where have I just come from? — anchoring a scene (Simson)
- **Q588** What qualities attract me to well-known actors? — surfacing taste before casting (Simson)
- **Q589** How do you want to make an audience feel? — the editing criterion for every cut (Simson)
- **Q590** Where do I start the scene as late as possible and stop as early as possible? — scene boundaries (Haven)
- **Q591** What does each speaker hope to say, conceal or notice; do I stay in one mind or hop between them; whose eyes serve best? — dialogue and perspective (Haven)
- **Q592** What do I see, hear, smell, touch and taste here, and where is it hardest to find vivid detail? — blank sensory spots as a research signal (Karia; Kamath)
- **Q593** Which single sense dominates this memory? — a dominant-sense anchor (Buster)
- **Q594** What does the audience need and want to hear, why do I care so much, and how can I pass the flame? — emotional transfer (Buster)
- **Q595** What emotions or what irony does the story evoke, and can I name them? — naming the feeling (Buster)
- **Q596** Does the story evoke wonder, and how do I imagine my own future? — wonder and vision (Buster)
- **Q597** What is the story about, the idea behind the episode, in a singular point of view? — theme for prep (Simson)
- **Q598** Where can the listener step inside as an ordinary person whose life is interrupted? — an entry point for listeners (Schwabauer)
- **Q599** Where does the internal context of the story overlap with the audience's lived experience? — resonance (Schwabauer)
- **Q600** How does the story twist in an unexpected direction? — pivot (James)
- **Q601** What does the reader's baseline expectation say, what is a logical twist, the opposite reversal, and what danger could strike? — the TRD options (Cleland)
- **Q602** Will this break add real value, or is it only shock; where does my pitch copy a template cliché? — structural twist test (Kamath)
- **Q603** Would you? You know how...? — a prompt that places the audience inside the protagonist's dilemma; first establish enough uncontested premises (Kamath)
- **Q604** I wonder...? — a curious opener that invites listeners to fill gaps without literalism (Miller)
- **Q605** What shape can you see, and how does my shape connect to yours? — building a scene collaboratively from clouds or objects (Miller)
- **Q606** How aged is this object, where did it originate, who owns it, and how is it more than it first seems? — turning a prop into an obstacle (Miller)
- **Q607** What is my precise intent for this story (bedtime, a transition, or calming a fear), and is my hesitation instinct or a lack of confidence? — purpose and self-censorship (Miller)
- **Q608** What is the real need behind the tears or screams: hunger, tiredness, overload, attention or a boundary? — diagnose a child's distress before choosing a story (Miller)
- **Q609** Is the child stuck asking what the toy does instead of what I can do with it; has he seen anyone use it; was it tried more than once? — why a story tool failed (Miller)
- **Q610** If I tell the story minus the prop, is anything lacking; does the prop steal focus from me; how do I signal when to join in and when to stop? — accessories gate (Haven)
