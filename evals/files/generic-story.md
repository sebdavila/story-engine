# Founder story draft

## Brief
- Audience: visitors to the About page of a specialty coffee roaster
- Objective: they should trust the brand enough to try a first bag
- Domain: brand
- Format: web page text
- Length: about 250 words

## Story

Our company was founded on a simple idea: everyone deserves great coffee. From the very beginning, we were passionate about quality and committed to excellence in everything we do.

Over the years, we have worked hard to build relationships with the best farms around the world. We believe that coffee is more than a drink. It is an experience that brings people together and creates moments that matter.

Our team is made up of dedicated professionals who share a love for the craft. We are constantly innovating and looking for new ways to deliver value to our customers. Sustainability is at the heart of our mission, and we strive to make a positive impact on the communities we serve.

We faced many challenges along the way, but our passion kept us going. Today, we are proud to serve thousands of happy customers who trust us for their daily cup. Every bag we roast reflects our values of integrity, quality and care.

We invite you to join our journey. Whether you are a casual drinker or a true connoisseur, we have something for you. Taste the difference that passion makes. Because at the end of the day, great coffee is not just what we do. It is who we are.
