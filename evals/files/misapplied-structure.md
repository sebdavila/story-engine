# Structure: renewal call with a hesitant hospital buyer

## Brief
- Audience: procurement lead at a regional hospital, renewing a scheduling software contract
- Objective: they should renew, despite a doubt they have not voiced (last year's outage)
- Domain: sales
- Goal map row: Defuse an unspoken objection
- Format: spoken, video call
- Length: 3 minutes

## Beats
1. Shared ground: the night-shift scramble every hospital knows when a nurse calls in sick.
2. Destination: next winter with zero unfilled shifts, and 6 hours a week back for the charge nurses.
3. Obstacles: paper swap requests, late approvals, staff who never see open shifts.
4. Make it real: one charge nurse's Monday, 5 a.m., filling two gaps from her phone in four minutes.
5. Close: growth for the ambitious board member, reliability for the cautious CFO.
