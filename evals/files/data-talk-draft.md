# Data talk draft

## Brief
- Audience: regional operations managers of a grocery chain, monthly review meeting
- Objective: approve moving the weekly produce delivery from Monday to Thursday
- Domain: data
- Format: 3-minute spoken update with two slides
- Length: about 350 words

## Draft

Good morning. Today I want to walk you through our produce waste numbers for the last quarter.

As you can see on this slide, total produce waste was 11.4% of produce sales, compared with 8.9% in the same quarter last year. Waste is highest for leafy greens (19%), berries (16%) and stone fruit (12%). Bananas and potatoes stayed below 5%.

We looked at waste by day of the week. Waste is concentrated on Sundays and Mondays, when stores discard product that arrived the previous Monday. Stores that receive two deliveries a week have 3 points less waste than stores with one.

We also looked at sales by day. About 41% of weekly produce sales happen between Thursday and Saturday. Monday deliveries mean produce sits for three or four days before the peak.

There are several factors to consider. Our supplier can deliver on Thursdays at no extra cost. Store teams would need to adjust their receiving shifts. Some stores have limited cold storage. A pilot in five stores would let us test the impact.

In summary, the data suggests that a Thursday delivery could reduce waste. We recommend considering a change to the delivery day. Let me know if you have any questions.
